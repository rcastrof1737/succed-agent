# Proyecto Faro: confidential teaser (SYNTHETIC DEMO DOCUMENT)

> This is a fictitious teaser for testing the screening-agent. It does not describe a real company.

**Page 1. Overview**

A founder-led environmental testing laboratory in Valencia, Spain, founded in 1994. It serves municipal water utilities and food producers under 2–3 year framework contracts. There are 62 employees, and an operations manager and a quality director report to the founder (age 66). The founder is willing to stay 9 months after the sale.

**Page 2. Financials (EUR millions; FX assumption for the demo: 1 EUR = 1.08 USD)**

| | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 9.1 | 9.8 | 10.6 |
| Reported EBITDA | 1.9 | 2.1 | 2.4 |
| Owner add-backs | 0.1 | 0.1 | 0.15 |
| Capex | 0.35 | 0.40 | 0.42 |

- About 70% of revenue comes from framework contracts or recurring sampling programmes.
- The largest customer (a regional water utility) is 14% of revenue.
- Accreditation is held by the company, not the founder.

**Page 3. Process**

The owner is seeking a successor rather than a financial sale. Indicative asking price: EUR 15M (enterprise value).
